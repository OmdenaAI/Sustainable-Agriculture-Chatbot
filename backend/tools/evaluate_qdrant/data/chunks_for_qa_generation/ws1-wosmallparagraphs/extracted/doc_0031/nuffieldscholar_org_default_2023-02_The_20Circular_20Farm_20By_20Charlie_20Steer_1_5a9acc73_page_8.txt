Objectives
Investigate how the use of the principles of the Circular Economy are incorporated into farming and
food production systems.
Promote the concept of the Circular Farm to communities involved in farming, land management
and food production.
Put into practice ideas and innovations gathered, in the management of Grosvenor Farms and
demonstrate the practicalities to other farmers and interested parties.
