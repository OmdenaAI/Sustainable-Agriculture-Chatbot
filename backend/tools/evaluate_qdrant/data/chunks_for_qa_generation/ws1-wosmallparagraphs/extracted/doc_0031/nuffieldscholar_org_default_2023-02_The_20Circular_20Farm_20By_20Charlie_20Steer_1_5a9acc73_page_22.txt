Lessons learnt from waste recycling, recovery and reuse case studies and the
implications for the Circular Farm
Discussing the opportunities for the Circular Farm with the innovators in the waste and nutrient
recovery and anaerobic digestion industries gave an exciting insight into the possibilities for circular
products highlighting additional revenue generation from wastes.
The businesses visited had one thing in common, vast amounts of capital requirements. From $17
million AD plants to biofertiliser plants starting at £2 million. High capital means that the creation of
circular farming systems using the solutions seen is not within easy reach for most farming
enterprises.
A way that capital for such schemes could be more easily in reach of farming business in the UK is
the formation of farmer and industry cluster groups to provide bio energy and bio fertiliser for their
own use and local demand.
