Your vision, mission and goals statements are likely to change as you
conduct research and develop an overall business strategy in Planning Task Four
(Strategic Planning and Evaluation). The Minars, for example, spent weeks on
their initial visioning process. However, after analyzing the feasibility of on-farm
processing and deciding to implement this strategy, they ultimately chose to
include only a short statement about the business component of their vision: “Our
vision is to process and direct market all of our milk and meat products. We would like
to help other farmers direct market their own products, possibly by creating a franchise
for milk processing plants.”
