Conclusion Sustainability is a guiding principle for Swiss agricultural policy.

Sustainability indicators are used to monitor agriculture from a sustain- ability perspective.First results show that on the whole trends in agricul- ture since 1990 have remained sustainable in those areas where agricul- tural policy has had an impact. Sustainable agriculture will continue to be a key policy objective. The agricultural policy package “Agricultural Policy 2011,” which was submitted to Parliament in 2006, represents a further important step.
