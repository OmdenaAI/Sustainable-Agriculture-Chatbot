Certain questions dealt with by the indicators in the socio-territorial scale can only be analyzed through qualitative factors.Quantifiable or observable items can nevertheless be combined with qualitative elements, as long as they have a meaning on the territorial scale.In this respect,the self-evalua- tion approach as proposed is a pragmatic way of assessing complex phe- nomena,and has its place in an awareness-raising approach.
