On the basis of their natural skills and feelings and/or long-term experi- ments, managers need methods and frames to help them make deci- sions and elaborate indicators that are appropriate for their firm (or farm). Therefore, indicators at firm- (or farm-) scale are useful if they are developed with managers and not prescribed from above in a top-down manner.
