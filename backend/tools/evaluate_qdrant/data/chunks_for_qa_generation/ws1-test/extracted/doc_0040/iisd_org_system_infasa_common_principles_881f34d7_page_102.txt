Agro-ecological sustainability scale This scale analyzes the propensity of the technical system to combine efficient use of the environment and the lowest possible ecological cost.

This first scale includes the indicators illustrating the capability of the farms to be more or less autonomous in relation to the use of non- renewable energy and materials and to generate more or less pollution.
