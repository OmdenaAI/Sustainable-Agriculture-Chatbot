Risk: To what type of risk is the business exposed?
In today’s agricultural industry, risk management has become an important
topic, particularly for those producers who have traditionally relied on government
intervention to mitigate price and production-related risks. Regardless of whether
you produce corn, soybeans, grass-based milk, organic vegetables or specialty fruit
trees, risk management will be an important component of your business. Without
it, uncontrolled risk and uncertainty reduce the reliability of financial projections
and make investment analysis difficult. Unmanaged risk also carries with it a high
degree of stress for farm managers and their families.
