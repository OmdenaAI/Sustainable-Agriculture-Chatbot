A significant change in the lives of people involved in a farming operation is often the factor that motivates the development of a new business plan. Dave and Florence Minar were motivated to explore on-farm milk processing, in part, as a way to create opportunities for their children to join the operation. Frank Foltz began to consider selling the catalog sales part of his business shortly after two daughters had married and moved away from home.
