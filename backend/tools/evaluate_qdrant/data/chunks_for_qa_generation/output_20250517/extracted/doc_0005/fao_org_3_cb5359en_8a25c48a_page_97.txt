87 Climate-smart agriculture (CSA) is a context- specific approach and includes three pillars: 1) sustainably increasing agricultural productivity and improving farmers’ incomes, 2)building resilience and adaptation to the effects of climate change, and, 3) where possible, reducing and/or removing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. ©FAO/ Hoang Dinh Nam
