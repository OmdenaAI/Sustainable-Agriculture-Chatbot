It is the intention of the International Forum on Assessing Sustainability in Agriculture (INFASA), to address the broad, worldwide use of indicator and assessment systems for sustainable agriculture.The pillars of science, policy and practice will form the basis of an ongoing dialogue on these tools to strategically advance the agricultural sustainability agenda by linking technical and policy aspects.

The primary objective of INFASA is to communicate the value of differ- ent indicator and assessment systems to potential users and to promote their use.To this end, INFASA aims to develop an intensive exchange of Sustainable Agriculture – From Common Principles to Common Practice 13
