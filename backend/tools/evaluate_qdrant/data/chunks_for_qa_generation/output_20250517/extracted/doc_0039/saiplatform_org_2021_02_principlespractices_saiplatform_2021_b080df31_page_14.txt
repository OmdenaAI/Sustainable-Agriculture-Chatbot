--- The Sustainable Agriculture Initiative Platform (SAI Platform) ---

The Sustainable Agriculture Initiative Platform (SAI Platform) is a global food and drink industry platform for developing sustainable agriculture solutions through member-driven pre-competitive collaboration. SAI Platform connects members and external stakeholders to catalyse change and establish sustainable agriculture as a pre-requisite for doing business throughout the food and drink supply chain. © SAI Platform, 2021 www.saiplatform.org
