This method is structured around objectives which are grouped together to form three sustainability scales.Each of these three scales is sub-divided into three or four components (making a total of 10 components) which in turn are made up of a total of 41 indicators.

The objectives of the agro-ecological scale refer to the agronomic prin- ciples of integrated agriculture (Viaux, 1999). They must enable good economic efficiency at as low as possible an ecological cost.Those of the socio-territorial sustainability scale refer more to ethics and human development, essential features of sustainable agricultural systems.

Lastly, the objectives of the economic sustainability scale specify the essential notions relating to the entrepreneurial function of the farm.

A single objective can contribute to the improvement of several com- ponents of sustainability.
