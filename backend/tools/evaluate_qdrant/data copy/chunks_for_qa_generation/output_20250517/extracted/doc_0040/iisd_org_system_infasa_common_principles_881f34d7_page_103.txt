Socio-territorial sustainability scale This scale characterizes the integration of the farm within its territory and in society. It seeks to assess the quality of life of the farmer and the weight of the market and non-market services rendered to the territory and to society. In this respect, it allows us to look into issues that go beyond the farm itself.
