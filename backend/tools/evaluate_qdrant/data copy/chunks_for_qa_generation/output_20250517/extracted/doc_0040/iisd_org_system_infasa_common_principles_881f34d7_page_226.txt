Future development of AEIs There are a number of issues concerning the future development of OECD AEIs. There is “unfinished business” related to existing indicators, including streamlining, (both through adding and subtracting from the current set of indicators), so that the indicators are better suited to policy needs and analytical efforts;and the need to better integrate and ensure consistency of indicators at different spatial levels, which would be helped by monetizing physical indicator data.
